Project 01 · Supply Chain Analytics

Supply Chain Delay Pattern Detector

Analysed 180,519 orders to find where deliveries fail, which routes need attention, and what is costing the business money. Built a weekly exception report that flags CRITICAL, WARNING and MONITOR routes for operations teams.

57.8% of all deliveries arrived late across 3 years of data
First Class shipping has a 100% late rate across all 22 regions with zero on-time deliveries
Standard Class, the cheapest option, is the most reliable at 39.8% late
No seasonal pattern found. Delays are structural, not demand-driven

Project 02 · Banking Analytics

Fidelis National Bank Customer Churn Analysis

Analysed 10,000 bank customers to find who is leaving and why. The finding no one expected: the customers leaving had more money than the ones who stayed. Built a Logistic Regression model and a 4-page Power BI dashboard with five retention recommendations.

20.4% overall churn rate. 1 in every 5 customers has left the bank
Churned customers held higher balances ($91,108) than those who stayed ($72,745)
Customers with 4 products had a 100% churn rate. Over-selling is driving exits
Age and active membership confirmed as the two strongest churn predictors

Project 03 · Fraud Analytics

FinLend Solutions Fraud Analytics

Built an end-to-end Power BI fraud analytics solution for FinLend Solutions, a fictional payments company operating across eight African markets, transforming 250,000 transactions into executive insight on where fraud is concentrated and which controls would have the greatest business impact.

Fraud rate rose 39% (2.39% to 3.32%) across $23.87M in transactions, with $917.58K confirmed fraudulent
Crypto Exchange merchants had the highest fraud rate at 10.12%, more than 3x the platform average
Accounts under 30 days old had a 6.23% fraud rate, almost 3x higher than mature accounts
