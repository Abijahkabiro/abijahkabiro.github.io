Project 03 · Supply Chain Analytics

Supply Chain Delay Pattern Detector

Analyzed 180,519 delivery records to identify operational bottlenecks, uncover delay drivers, and build a reporting solution that helps operations teams prioritize corrective action.

57.8% of deliveries arrived late across three years of operational data, highlighting a significant network performance challenge
First Class shipping recorded a 100% late delivery rate across all 22 regions, indicating a structural service performance issue
Standard Class, the lowest-cost shipping option, delivered the strongest performance with the lowest late delivery rate at 39.8%
No seasonal pattern was identified, suggesting delays were driven by operational factors rather than demand fluctuations
